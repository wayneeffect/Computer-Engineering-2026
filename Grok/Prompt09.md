**Here’s a list of notable talent/staffing agencies specializing in or strongly supporting computer engineering roles** (including software engineering, hardware/embedded engineering, computer systems, DevOps, AI/ML, and related tech positions). These firms handle contract, contract-to-hire, and permanent placements.

### Major National/Well-Known Agencies
- **Robert Half Technology** — A leading IT and tech staffing firm offering temporary, contract, and permanent placements for software engineers, systems roles, and broader tech positions. Strong in AI-powered matching and has extensive market data.
- **TEKsystems** — One of the largest IT staffing firms, placing thousands of professionals annually in engineering, software development, infrastructure, and related roles. Good for high-volume and enterprise needs.
- **Insight Global** — Known for rapid placements (sometimes 24-48 hours) with a large candidate database. Focuses on developers and engineers, often with flexible pay-after-hire models.
- **Apex Systems** — Specializes in tech and engineering staffing, including contract and direct hire for computer engineering roles.
- **KORE1** — Tech-focused recruiter with strong performance in AI/ML, cloud, and engineering placements. Often noted for faster time-to-fill in mid-market roles.

### Specialized or Boutique Agencies
- **Protingent** — Engineering recruiting firm strong in software, hardware, embedded systems, avionics, and related computer engineering disciplines. Active in Seattle, Portland, and Silicon Valley.
- **VALiNTRY** — Provides dedicated computer engineering staffing and recruitment services across the USA.
- **Redfish Technology** — Long-established (since 1996) specialist in software development, engineering, and IT talent. Covers SaaS, cloud, full-stack, and related areas.
- **Motion Recruitment** — Focuses on mid-to-senior level IT and tech roles, with strong presence in major cities like NYC and Los Angeles.
- **Halian** — Offers software engineering recruitment (full-stack, mobile, architects, DevOps, etc.) with options for permanent, freelance, and RPO services.
- **TRIAD Engineering Corp.** — Massachusetts-based firm specializing in hardware/embedded, software/firmware, systems, and broader engineering staffing (contract and direct hire).
- **Scion Technology** — Specializes in IT and engineering recruiting, with locations including San Diego and Stamford, CT. Good for tech talent in science/tech intersections.
- **DevsData** — NYC-focused IT staffing with emphasis on top software engineers and tech talent.

### Other Notable Mentions
- **Actalent** — Strong in engineering and specialized technical roles.
- **Kforce**, **Beacon Hill**, and **Mondo** — Frequently ranked in tech recruiting lists for IT and engineering.
- Regional or niche players like **Alpha Apex Group**, **Search Solution Group**, **CivicMinds** (California), and **Near** (for LatAm talent) may also be relevant depending on location and needs.

**Tips for choosing**:
- For **hardware/embedded/computer systems** roles, prioritize specialists like Protingent or TRIAD.
- For **software/full-stack/DevOps**, broader IT firms like Robert Half, TEKsystems, or Insight Global often work well.
- Consider your location (many have national reach but stronger networks in tech hubs), role level (mid-senior vs. high-volume), and engagement type (contract vs. permanent).
- Check current reviews on sites like Glassdoor, Clutch, or ClearlyRated, and verify their vetting processes.
